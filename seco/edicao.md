## [World] Human Rights Lawyer Wins Nobel Peace Prize
Navi Pillay, a South African lawyer, won the Nobel Peace Prize. She worked as a judge in South Africa and international courts. She chaired a UN panel that accused Israel of genocide in Gaza. The panel's findings were rejected by the Israeli government. She served as UN human rights chief and is now an ICJ judge. The US imposed sanctions on the International Criminal Court. Pillay was the first non-white female judge of the High Court of South Africa.

## [Culture] Canadian Poet Anne Carson Wins Nobel Prize
Anne Carson won the Nobel Prize in Literature this year. Carson is known for her bold and inventive works. She often mixes poetry, fiction, and scholarship. The Swedish Academy praised Carson's playful dialogue with classical tradition. Carson has written more than twenty books, including poetry and essays. She teaches classics, comparative literature, and creative writing at universities.

Fontes: The New York Times, BBC, The Guardian, DW

## [Space & Earth] Floods hit Thailand, affecting millions
Floods hit Thailand, affecting over 4 million people. Bangkok received nearly 300 millimeters of rain in 48 hours. Floods damaged about 270,000 hectares of farmland. The International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies provided aid. NASA images show flooded areas near the Bang Pakong River. Some flooding resulted from a low-pressure system that stalled over the area. The system delivered especially intense rains to the region. Floods occurred in 50 provinces, causing dozens of deaths. Floods also affected agricultural areas and aquaculture facilities.

Fontes: NASA
